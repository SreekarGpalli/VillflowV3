How to use
  1. Click in any text field (e.g. Notepad).
  2. Hold Ctrl+Shift+Z, speak, release — polished text is pasted at the cursor.
  3. Hold Ctrl+Shift+X for Command: rewrite a selection, or generate text when nothing is selected.
  4. Ctrl+Shift+C toggles the Scratchpad.
  5. Keys stay in %APPDATA%\VillFlow\ on this PC only.
